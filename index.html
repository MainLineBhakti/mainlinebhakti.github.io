The Archer's Practice

➤

Inspired by Arjuna from the Bhagavad Gita, our brand's arrow represents the deep connection between yoga sadhana and daily life.

The quiet, disciplined drawing back of the bow is our daily sadhana—our meditation and practice. It is this intentional pulling inward that provides the strength, focus, and clarity to let the arrow fly, allowing us to take deliberate aim for the day ahead with a heart full of devotion.
